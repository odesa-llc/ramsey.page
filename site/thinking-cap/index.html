Thinking cap.

An old sweater, turned into a fitted beanie. My plan for making one, following wynunlimited’s tutorial.

watch the tutorial ↗get the pattern ↗

Print the pattern at 100% on A4 or Letter. Check the 2 in / 5 cm square, then choose a size from the head measurements. Try a test first: knits stretch differently.

Use an old knit sweater, or about ¼ metre of 60-inch-wide knit. Cut four pieces on the fold, with the stretch running across the head. Snip tiny 2 mm notches at both ends of each fold.

Pair the pieces, right sides together. Match the tips and centre notches; sew one curved side of each pair at ¼ in (6 mm). Backstitch both ends. Sew slowly without stretching; the tutorial uses a 2.5 mm stitch. Press the seams open to reduce bulk.

Open both pairs and place them right sides together. Match the seams and side notches, then pin around the edge. Leave a 2 in / 5 cm turning gap near the inside crown. Sew the rest of the perimeter at ¼ in, backstitching at the gap.

Turn through the gap and stitch it closed. Tuck that half inside the other, matching the crown seams. Gently press the bottom fold. Optional: hand-tack the two crowns together so the layers stay put in the wash.
