Skull cap.

I turned an old knit sweater into a fitted skull cap, using wynunlimited’s four-panel construction and adding cream barbed-wire embroidery.

construction reference ↗pattern PDF ↗

I started with the tutorial’s pattern and an old knit sweater. The fabric’s stretch ran around the head; four folded pieces gave me both the outer cap and its lining.

I joined the panels in pairs, then pressed the seams open. I wanted a close fit with subtle joins that still showed how the cap was put together.

Joining the pairs created one double-layered shape. A small opening near the inner crown let me turn it right side out without leaving an opening along the hem.

I tucked the lining inside and matched the crown seams. A light press settled the bottom edge, while a small stitch between the crowns kept the layers together.

The cream barbed wire became the main detail: two wandering lines, dense wraps of different widths, and slightly uneven Xs. I kept it on the outer layer so the cap could still stretch.

Matte Titanium White Shiva Paintstik is my choice for the chalky finish: lightly brushed over the knit, then dried and heat-set within the fabric’s care limits.
